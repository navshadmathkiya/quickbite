Meet Our Team

The people behind FreshMarket's success

Navshad Mathakiya

Founder & CEO

My Journey

Growing up in Ahmedabad, I witnessed my mother spending hours every week at the local vegetable market, bargaining for the best prices. Despite her efforts, we were still paying much more than the wholesale rates available at the market yards.

This realization struck me when I started managing my own household - why couldn't regular families access wholesale market prices? That's when the idea of FreshMarket was born.

With a background in business and a passion for solving real problems, I decided to bridge this gap. What started as a small operation with a single vehicle has now grown to serve over 5,000 families across Ahmedabad.

My Vision

I believe that fresh, healthy food should be affordable and accessible to everyone. Our mission at FreshMarket is not just about delivering vegetables - it's about empowering families to eat healthier without breaking the bank.

Every day, I personally visit the wholesale market with our team, select the freshest produce, and ensure that quality is never compromised. Your trust drives us to do better every single day.
