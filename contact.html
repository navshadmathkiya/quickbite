Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What are your delivery areas?

We currently deliver to all major areas of Ahmedabad including Satellite, Maninagar, Vastrapur, Bopal, and more. Check our service areas on the "How It Works" page.

What is the minimum order value?

Currently, there is no minimum order value. Order as little or as much as you need!

How fresh are the products?

We purchase fresh produce from the wholesale market every morning at 5 AM and deliver the same day. Freshness is guaranteed!

What if I'm not satisfied with the quality?

Customer satisfaction is our top priority. If you're not happy with any product, contact us immediately and we'll replace it or provide a full refund.

Do you accept online payments?

Currently, we accept Cash on Delivery (COD). Online payment options (UPI, Cards) will be available soon!

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes! You can modify or cancel your order by calling us or messaging on WhatsApp before we start preparing it.
