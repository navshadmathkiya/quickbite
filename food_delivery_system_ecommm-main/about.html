About FreshMarket

Revolutionizing the way Ahmedabad buys fresh produce

Our Story

FreshMarket was born from a simple observation: families in Ahmedabad were paying high prices for vegetables and fruits at local retail markets, while the same products were available at wholesale market yards at much lower prices.

The problem? Individual consumers couldn't access wholesale markets. They had to buy at retail prices, paying 30-40% more for the same fresh produce.

We decided to bridge this gap. By purchasing directly from wholesale market yards and delivering to your doorstep, we eliminate the middleman and pass the savings directly to you.

Our Mission

To make fresh, high-quality vegetables and fruits accessible to every household in Ahmedabad at wholesale prices, delivered conveniently to their doorstep.

We believe that every family deserves access to affordable, fresh produce without compromising on quality or convenience.

Our Vision

To become Ahmedabad's most trusted fresh produce delivery service, expanding to cities across Gujarat and eventually India, making wholesale-priced fresh food accessible to millions of families.
